About Darie Mae Robinson YPD

Grow, Glow and Go for Christ

Who Are We?🤷‍♀️

DMR YPD is a youth ministry dedicated to empowering young people to Grow, Glow and Go for Christ. As part of the Young People's and Children's Division (YPD) of the Women's Missionary Society of the African Methodist Episcopal Church (AME).

Our History📜

The YPD has a long history within the AME Church. The AME Church was formally established in 1816 under the leadership of Richard Allen and it grew into a global church with a strong commitment to faith, education, leadership and service.The YPD was formally adopted within the missionary work of the AME in the early twentieth century developing programmes that gave young people opportunities to learn, serve, lead and become more involved in church life. Its purpose continues to include youth training, Christian education, leadership development and mission work.DMR YPD carries the legacy forward by creating opportunities for young people to connect, worship, learn, serve and grow together. We believe that young people are not just the future. We are an important part of the church today.

Our Mission🤍

To foster a personal relationship with Jesus Christ among young people, Discipleship and spiritual growth, Leadership development and community service.

Our Mission Statement🤍

As a unified body with Christlike qualities, we will build a connectional bond that demonstrates pride through education,communities and mission work.

Vision✨

Strongly founded on scriptures, Dedicated to ministering to the Christian Faith, Competent to lead within the society and the church.

Motto💡

"Grow, Glow and Go for Christ!"
