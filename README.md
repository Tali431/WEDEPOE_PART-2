### Name:

### Brief History:
There is a division of youth and children within the Women's Missionary Society (WMS) of the African Methodist Episcopal (AME) Church known as the Connectional Young People's and Children's Division (YPD). The main purpose of this division is the development of youths in their ethical, academic, social, and spiritual fields. (African Methodist Episcopal Church, 2019) The African Methodist Episcopal Church is one that was formed by people of African descent(namely Richard Allen) in the United States in 1816. This church has been formed for the advancement of spirituality, education, social justice, and human dignity. The formation of the Women’s Missionary Society (WMS) is meant to coordinate missionary work, evangelism, education, and charitable activities as the Church expanded. (Norris, 2016)The WMS formed the Young People’s and Children’s Division (YPD) for the purpose of involving children and youth in the work and ministries of the Church since they realized the significance of grooming future leaders.

### Timeline and Milestones
The project will begin with **project planning and research**, where DMRobinson AME YPD will be selected as the organisation and approval will be obtained from the lecturer. This will be followed by **research and information gathering**, which will involve collecting relevant information about the YPD, including its history, mission, vision, activities, target audience and contact details. The next stage will focus on **website planning**, where the website objectives, target audience, pages, features and key performance indicators (KPIs) will be finalised. Once the planning stage is complete, **content and page structure** will be developed by finalising the Home, About Us, Services/Programs, Gallery, Enquiries and Contact pages. The project will then move into **design and wireframes**, where low-fidelity wireframes will be completed and the YPD's green, black and white colour palette, typography and overall layout will be finalised. During the **HTML development** stage, the website pages will be created and a navigation system will be established to allow users to move easily between the different pages. The following stage will involve **content implementation**, where text, images, YPD information, events, contact details and enquiry forms will be added to the website. **CSS and visual design** will then be applied to create a consistent appearance across the website, including the YPD colour scheme, fonts, spacing, buttons, navigation and page styling. After development, **testing and corrections** will be conducted to ensure that all links, forms, images and navigation work correctly, while spelling, formatting and HTML/CSS errors will also be corrected. The project will then proceed to **user experience testing**, where the website will be checked to ensure that it is easy to navigate and functions correctly across different screen sizes. **Final improvements** will be made based on the results of testing as well as feedback received from the lecturer and peers. The final stages will involve **documentation**, where the proposal or process document, references, screenshots and supporting evidence will be completed. Finally, **submission preparation** will be completed by conducting a final quality check, organising all files and folders, preparing the required submission package and submitting the completed website project.
